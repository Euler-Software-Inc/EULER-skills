EULER skills for Claude

Partner-relationship workflows — QBRs, pre-call briefings, whole-portfolio pulse, referrals — as one-command Claude skills, built on the EULER MCP. Every skill returns a single self-contained HTML report styled with the EULER design system: open it in a browser, print to PDF, or share the file.

EULER for Partner Managers

euler-for-partner-managers

Customer-admin workflows: generate a QBR, prep for a partner call, read the whole portfolio, and triage your approval queue.

EULER for Partners

euler-for-partners

Partner self-service: your performance scorecard, onboarding & certification progress, referral and deal history, and submitting a new referral.

Install

Add the marketplace, install your plugin

Inside Claude Code, add the marketplace once, then install whichever plugin fits your role.

# add the marketplace once
/plugin marketplace add Euler-Software-Inc/EULER-skills

# for partner-relationship managers (customer-admin)
/plugin install euler-for-partner-managers@euler-plugins

# for partners (self-service)
/plugin install euler-for-partners@euler-plugins

First invocation triggers a one-time OAuth consent against mcp.eulerapp.com (token cached 30 days). Skills run under each plugin's namespace, e.g. /euler-for-partner-managers:generate-qbr.

Skills

Nine skills across two plugins

For partner managers — /euler-for-partner-managers

generate-qbr

:generate-qbr

A Quarterly Business Review for a specific partner — performance, pipeline, referrals, agreements, health score.

partner-briefing

:partner-briefing

A 30-second pre-call read: what they'll want to discuss, what you should bring up, last-30-day stats.

portfolio-pulse

:portfolio-pulse

One screen across all partners — totals, top performers, who needs attention, coverage gaps. Scales to tens of thousands.

pending-approvals-triage

:pending-approvals-triage

An age-sorted worklist of everything awaiting your approval — partner apps, referrals, deal registrations.

For partners — /euler-for-partners

my-performance

:my-performance

Your performance scorecard with a customer — health score (0–100), revenue, deals, commissions, referrals, and the biggest lever to improve.

my-onboarding

:my-onboarding

Your onboarding and certification progress across assigned flows — what's done, what's left, what's overdue.

my-referrals

:my-referrals

Your full referral history with a customer — status of everything you submitted, sorted by recency.

my-deals

:my-deals

Your deal registrations — status breakdown, per-deal value and stage, total attributed pipeline.

submit-a-referral

:submit-a-referral

Submit a new referral through chat — collects the details interactively, then records it via the EULER MCP.

How it works

Curated workflows on the EULER MCP

The plugins don't ship their own server — they orchestrate the existing remote EULER MCP.

1

You invoke a skill; Claude reads its playbook (SKILL.md).

2

Claude connects to mcp.eulerapp.com (OAuth 2.1, one-time consent).

3

It orchestrates the MCP tools per the skill's instructions.

4

It renders the result as a self-contained HTML report.
